HOBBIES

My hobbies include speedcubing, beyblades, and video games. I like doing other things in my free time as well, but this is what I am doing most often.

SPEEDCUBING

Speedcubing is the practice of solving a Rubik's cube as quickly as possible. I personally am not the fastest speedsolver, but I can solve a few types of cubes decently fast. The main ones that I solve are the 3x3x3, 2x2x2, and 4x4x4 cubes. Others I solve include the 5x5x5 and mirror cubes.

Here are some of my recorded times:

  • 3x3x3 Cube - 12.46 seconds
  • 2x2x2 Cube - 16.67 seconds
  • 4x4x4 Cube - 49.82 seconds

  • Mirror Cube - N/A
